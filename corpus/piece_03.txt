In the 12 months to June 2025, 23% of our product returns came from a single problem: texture and separation in one moisturiser. When we looked at where those returns came from, 71% were from customers in cities where humidity regularly sits above 70%.

It took us embarrassingly long to make the connection. We had treated each return as an individual complaint, replaced the product, and moved on. It was only when we mapped returns against cities that the pattern stopped looking like bad luck.

The formula was not faulty in the way a lab would define it. It passed its stability tests. But those tests were run at conditions that do not describe a monsoon in Mumbai or Chennai, and the emulsion was more sensitive to sustained heat and moisture than the data suggested. That is not a flattering thing to admit. It is what happened.

We reformulated, and we changed how we test, so humidity is now part of the question from the start rather than something we notice in the returns data.

I'm not sharing this to say our products are now perfect. I'm sharing it because India is a hard climate for skincare, and very little of the testing behind what you buy was designed with it in mind. If you live somewhere humid and a product keeps separating or changing texture, it is not your storage. Ask the brand whether it was ever tested in conditions like yours.
