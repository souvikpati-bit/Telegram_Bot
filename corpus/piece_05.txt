I want to be direct about something before this list gets any bigger. This list is not going to be a place where I sell you things.

When I started Skinstinct, the thing that frustrated me most about skincare was not bad products. It was the distance between what a label said and what the formula actually did, and how little of that distance a customer could see. Most brands, including good ones, communicate in claims. I would rather communicate in reasons.

So what you will get here is the reasoning. Why an ingredient behaves the way it does. What a percentage on a label can and cannot tell you. Where the testing behind a product tends to fall short for people living in Indian heat and humidity. Where we have got things wrong ourselves, with the numbers attached, because it would be odd to ask you for that kind of honesty from other brands and not offer it.

When I don't know something, I'll say so. When the evidence for a popular claim is thinner than the marketing suggests, I'll say that too, even when it is inconvenient for us.

If that is not what you signed up for, the unsubscribe link will not hurt my feelings. If it is, I am glad you are here.
